## 3. Scope of Work

### Interior Unit Scope (per unit)

For each of the 8 units (101–104, 201–204), Contractor shall perform:

- Remove existing carpet and sheet vinyl; prepare subfloor and substrates as needed.  
- Install new LVP flooring in living, dining, kitchen, and halls; install new carpet in bedrooms.  
- Repaint all walls, ceilings, doors, and trim with the agreed color scheme.  
- Replace or reface kitchen cabinets per plan; install new laminate or quartz countertops.  
- Replace kitchen sink, faucet, and garbage disposal as needed.  
- Install new range hood and kitchen lighting; replace or reinstall appliances as specified.  
- Replace bathroom vanity, countertop, sink, faucet, toilet, and tub/shower trim; repair or retile tub surround as required.  
- Replace interior door hardware and selected light fixtures.  
- Perform minor electrical and plumbing work associated with the above improvements.

### Building and Common-Area Scope

For the building and shared spaces, Contractor shall perform:

- Refinish and repaint interior common corridors and stairwells.  
- Upgrade corridor and exterior entry lighting.  
- Perform minor concrete patching at walks and entries.  
- Repair select exterior trim and repaint targeted siding/soffit areas.  
- Upgrade mail area and install new property signage as specified.

Items expressly excluded unless added by change order include HVAC replacement, full roof replacement, major structural repairs, full window replacement, substantial site work, and any scope not described in this section or in the attached proposal.

## 7. Payment Schedule and Retainage

Payments shall be made via check or ACH to Sonoran Build & Repair LLC at the Contractor address above.

### Retainage

Retainage on construction projects is commonly negotiated in the **5%–10%** range of each progress payment, with exact terms set in the contract.[web:70][web:119][web:120][web:144] For this Project:

- The standard retainage rate shall be **ten percent (10%)** of each progress payment, which falls within the typical 5%–10% range used on private multifamily projects.[web:70][web:119][web:144]  
- The parties may agree in writing to adjust the retainage percentage to any value between **five percent (5%)** and **ten percent (10%)**, provided such adjustment complies with applicable law and is documented by written amendment to this Contract.[web:70][web:108][web:119][web:124][web:125]

Retainage shall be calculated as a percentage of the amount earned on each approved invoice and deducted from the amount otherwise payable, and shall accumulate until released as described in “Retainage Release” below.[web:70][web:119][web:120][web:144]

### Retainage Release

1. The Owner shall release **half of the retainage** held to date upon achievement of **Substantial Completion** of all 8 units and interior common corridors, subject to delivery of conditional lien waivers for the related work.  
2. The Owner shall release the **remaining retainage** upon **Final Completion**, including completion of punch‑list items, delivery of final unconditional lien waivers from Contractor and major subcontractors, and submission of warranties and closeout documentation reasonably required under this Contract.[web:70][web:119][web:120][web:124][web:144]  
3. Retainage terms shall comply with any applicable state retainage limits or prompt‑payment requirements.

Invoices are due within **15 calendar days** of receipt unless otherwise specified. Late payments may accrue interest at the lesser of **1.0% per month** or the maximum amount allowed by law.
